**Scope:** Local repository sources plus public App Store and Apple Developer pages. No App Store Connect data or controls were accessed. No build, test, or device run was performed for this audit.

Status describes the evidence available here: **Supported** means the local source/docs corroborate the scoped claim; **Unsupported** means the claim has no evidence in the reviewed material or is contradicted; **Unverified** means this audit cannot establish it, usually because runtime, device, or account data is needed. “Supported” is not a claim that the current shipped binary was independently tested.

## Accessibility label evidence needed

Apple’s [Accessibility Nutrition Labels guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/overview-of-accessibility-nutrition-labels) says a feature should be indicated only when users can complete **all common tasks** with that feature. It calls out the first-launch experience and Settings as common tasks, in addition to the app’s primary functionality, and says the evaluation should be performed for each device the app supports. Apple recommends a task-by-feature matrix for each device. The product-page section can remain visible with “not indicated” when a developer has not supplied responses.

For ForzAdvisor, a practical iPhone common-task set should cover first launch/empty Garage; starting a tune through manual entry and photo or screenshot import; reviewing and correcting detected/input values; choosing game, discipline, and provider and generating; reading, saving, copying, and refining a result; finding, editing, and deleting a saved tune with Undo; and opening Settings to inspect provider/privacy state. Include camera/OCR and the system photo picker where they are part of the advertised paths. There is no login or purchase flow to assess.

Before indicating a feature, evidence should show that every applicable task remains perceivable, operable, and understandable with that feature enabled, on each supported device. The existing spec records Accessibility XXXL and Increase Contrast captures and automated hierarchy checks, but those do not by themselves establish complete label support. Reconcile the conflicting VoiceOver notes against the exact build and retained evidence. Add a fresh VoiceOver order/focus/announcement pass and a device run for camera/OCR; evaluate any proposed Larger Text, Voice Control, contrast, color-differentiation, dark-interface, or reduced-motion label against Apple’s feature-specific criteria. The App Store’s “Only for iPhone” label is the present target signal; confirm the supported-device scope before applying Apple’s per-device rule to any compatibility path.

## PPO and App Analytics: deferred, conditional

No Product Page Optimization test was run, and no App Store Connect metrics were viewed in this audit. A public ratings overview is not a substitute for acquisition, conversion, or usage metrics.

Apple’s [PPO analytics guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/product-page-optimization) says a test appears in Analytics after at least **five first-time downloads** are attributed to it. That is a **data-availability/display threshold**, not evidence that a result is sufficiently precise to guide a decision. Apple reports treatment confidence and marks “Performing Better” or “Performing Worse” at **90% confidence**; it can mark a test “Likely to be Inconclusive” if results are unlikely to become significant within 90 days. A useful decision still needs enough impressions/downloads for the specific effect being tested, a clearly defined baseline and conversion outcome, and uncertainty narrow enough for the owner’s decision. Five downloads alone is not adequate inference.

Apple defines App Store conversion rate from downloads and unique impressions; its App Analytics overview also says in-app engagement data includes only users who agreed to share diagnostics and usage information ([App Analytics](https://developer.apple.com/app-store-connect/analytics/)). Keep acquisition metrics and opted-in usage metrics distinct. Revisit PPO or other App Analytics only if authorized access is available, observed traffic can support a meaningful test, and a single hypothesis, treatment, metric, and decision rule are specified. Do not infer competitor superiority from the public listing or from low-volume results.
