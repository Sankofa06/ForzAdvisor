# Shared Memory

This is curated, advisory memory for evidence-backed decisions and reusable lessons. Current user intent, `AGENTS.md`, and authoritative repository evidence always outrank it.

## Limits and evidence standards

- Durable memory: at most 25 active entries; review every quarter.
- Working memory: at most 10 active entries; expire after 30 days unless renewed from current evidence.
- Every entry must be reusable, scoped, evidence-backed, and include its source/date, confidence, last verification, and a review, expiry, promotion, or deletion trigger.
- Clearly distinguish observed fact from inference; revalidate an entry before relying on it.
- Secrets, raw transcripts, hidden reasoning, sensitive personal information, private infrastructure identifiers, and unsupported reviewer taste are prohibited.

## Maintenance

At the start of relevant work, retrieve only entries whose scope matches and revalidate them against current evidence. Mark contradicted entries stale immediately. Delete expired working entries; do not archive them as a transcript. Promote a working entry only after it changes a repeated future decision or is clearly causal in one high-impact case. Delete or revise durable entries when their review trigger fires or current evidence supersedes them.
